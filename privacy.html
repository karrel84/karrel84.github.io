Privacy Policy — Karrel Shorts Uploader

Last updated: 2026-11-12

What the app accesses

The app requests the YouTube scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly. It uses them only to upload videos to the signed-in channel and to read that channel's name and the status of uploaded videos.

How data is stored

The OAuth token is saved only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server. It does not collect, sell, or share any user data with third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/karrel84/karrel84.github.io.
